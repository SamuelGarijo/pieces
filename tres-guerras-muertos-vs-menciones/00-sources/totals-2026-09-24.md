# Cumulative death tolls, as of September 2026 — for the "total victims vs media presence" frame

Gathered 2026-09-24 by Claude via web search; each figure labelled. Categories are NOT
homogeneous across wars — the frame must say which count is which.

## Ukraine (since 24 Feb 2022)
- **Civilians killed: 16,874** (OHCHR/HRMMU, verified, to end of July 2026; "actual figures may
  be higher"). Source: ukraine.ohchr.org, Protection of Civilians updates 2026. VERIFIED (UN).
- Military: Ukrainian fatalities 100,000–140,000 (CSIS, Jan 2026, Feb 2022–Dec 2025);
  Russian fatalities 352,000 identified by name (Mediazona/Meduza, May 2026) to
  400,000–500,000 (CSIS 2026; UK intelligence May 2026; Cavoli Jul 2026). ESTIMATES.
- All deaths, both sides, order of magnitude: ~470,000–660,000. ESTIMATE, wide.

## Gaza (since 7 Oct 2023)
- **73,910 killed** (Gaza Ministry of Health, 19 Sep 2026; via palinfo/MEE). Includes
  combatants; the IDF has accepted the ministry's count (Yahoo/Times of Israel report, 71,000).
  UN OCHA July 2026: >73,000 incl. 1,700 health workers. OFFICIAL COUNT.
- Lancet Global Health 2025 field survey and Al Jazeera Feb 2026 ("exceeds 75,000, independent
  data verify") suggest the count is an undercount. ESTIMATE ABOVE.

## Sudan (since 15 Apr 2023)
- **≥ 58,000 reported fatalities** (ACLED, via CSIS "The Sudan War in 10 Charts", 17 Apr 2026;
  "the actual number is certainly much higher"). RECORDED MINIMUM.
- LSHTM (Nov 2024, Lancet Global Health 2025): Khartoum State alone, Apr 2023–Jun 2024:
  >61,000 all-cause excess deaths, >26,000 violent; >90 % unrecorded. ESTIMATE, one state.
- 150,000 (ReliefWeb situation analysis, Mar 2026; earlier US envoy Perriello 2024) to
  400,000 (NYT opinion, Aug 2025). ESTIMATES, national.

## Reading
- Counting soldiers, Ukraine is by far the deadliest.
- Counting civilians only, Ukraine's verified figure is the lowest; Gaza's official count is
  the best-documented large number; Sudan's is a range whose top end is the largest of all
  and whose width IS the finding: the least counted war.
