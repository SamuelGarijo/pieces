## Frame 2 — the risk, stated

A "whiteness scale" drawn literally reads as the thing it mocks once it leaves the
carousel. The draft therefore plots **the quotes**, not skin: the journalists wrote the
scale themselves, on air; the frame only arranges what they said. Irony mark in text; the
self-directed line keeps the irony pointed inward (Samuel's rule). Verify exact wording
and dates before publishing — see `00-sources`.
