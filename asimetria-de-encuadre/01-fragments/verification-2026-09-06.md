## Consequences for the piece

- Every figure in the AI summary needed correction. None was fabricated; all were
  compressed in a way a hostile reader would catch. The draft's rule stands: primaries only.
- F04 is unblocked — and sharper: the BBC's own guidance says "terrorist" only with
  attribution, and its pages say it 560 times. CNN goes the other way, which the piece must
  say if it names the BBC (draft counter-argument #2: don't generalize).
- F05's strongest number is CfMM's 1,155 vs 119 — the tag on the number, counted directly.
- F02 cannot claim a two-sided ratio. It can *show* the mechanism (a sentence with no
  subject) and cite NewsCord's 77% for one side, with the LLM-coding caveat.
- F03's object is an eight-page form with a censor clause. Eight is drawable; eleven would
  have been the piece's first factual error in a piece about rigor.
