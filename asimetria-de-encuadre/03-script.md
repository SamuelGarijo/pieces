Method: `content-creation-engine/references/` — `script-schema.md` (the fields),

### Pacing checks (three, one beat list)

- **Kernel ratio** (Chatman): 3 of 10 (frames 6, 7, 10) — inside the 2–3 default. ✓
- **Tempo profile** (Genette): pause · summary · scene · scene · scene · pause · pause · scene
  · summary · pause. Longest run of one value = 3 (frames 3–5, `scene`). ✓ No flatline.
- **Closure budget** (McCloud, notebook weights: moment 1 · action 2 · subject 3 · aspect 4
  · scene 5): 5+5+3+3+5+1+5+3+4 = **34 against a 24 default.** ⚠️ Over. Two of the
  scene-to-scene jumps are structural (1→2 the hole, 5→6 the BBC → CNN/MSNBC change of
  sample) and one is avoidable: **7→8 becomes subject-to-subject** by keeping the desk of
  frame 7 and putting the form on it (same setting, new object) — 34 → 32. Still over.
  Reading: the budget was calibrated on a story carousel; a coverage-pattern piece is
  *made* of matched pairs, i.e. subject-to-subject steps at weight 3, so its floor is
  higher. **Logged as the first calibration case** (`visual-rhetoric.md`, closure budget:
  "a placeholder until real pieces calibrate it") — the recommendation is not to cut
  frames but to set the coverage-pattern default at ~32.

## The beats

Each beat: what is on the frame, what the caption says (draft, Spanish), what is
withheld, and the pointer to the fragment. The illustration brief (`04-brief.md`) is
built from these; here only what the brief needs to know.

### 1 — Threshold. The law, as two shelves.
**On the frame:** two identical shelves, side by side (`paralelismo`). One is full of
folders; the other has one folder and a gap. No labels yet. Newsprint grey; nothing in
colour.
**Caption:** *Un niño muerto en Ucrania recibió 16,1 menciones en CNN y MSNBC. Un niño
muerto en Gaza, 0,36. Mismos cien días.* — F01, verified (CNN+MSNBC combined; the
per-child ratio, with the raw pair held back for frame 7).
**Withheld:** the raw mentions (4,223 / 3,632) — the `late-fact`.
**Why here:** verdict-first (The Nation). The reader's hypothesis forms: "they cover them
differently". The `hole` is planted as a gap on a shelf.

### 2 — The hook. Eight years, fourteen thousand, nobody knows.
**On the frame:** the same shelf, now empty end to end (`elipsis-fondo`: no wall, no
floor — the shelf floats). Right-to-left composition (analepsis, reach 8 years). Grey.
**Caption:** *Donbás, 2014–2022. Ocho años. Unos 14.000 muertos. Casi nadie con quien
hablas sabe que ocurrió.* — F06, OHCHR.
**Withheld:** any image of the war.
**Why here:** the draft's opening, placed second: the hole proves the premise (attention ≠
magnitude) before the piece enters its case. "Sin acusar a nadie todavía. Sólo el hueco."

### 3 — Pair 1. The same verb, and the other verb.
**On the frame:** two BBC-style headlines set in the same typeface, same width, one above
the other (`antitesis`). Top: a sentence with a subject and "killed". Bottom: a sentence
with "died" and no subject — the subject's place is a visible blank. Grey.
**Caption:** *En un año de BBC online: "killed", 1.167 veces para muertos palestinos y
1.163 para israelíes — con 34 veces más muertos en Gaza. "Died": 50 frente a 7.* — F02
via CfMM, verified.
**Withheld:** the actor. The blank is the point.
**Why here:** the case opens on the sentence, because the sentence is the smallest unit of
the mechanism. `now` = frame 3.

### 4 — Pair 2. The tag.
**On the frame:** two numbers, large, same size, same grey. On one of them a small
two-word sticker — **the only colour in the piece** — reading *Hamas-run*. The other
number bare.
**Caption:** *"Hamas-run health ministry": 1.155 artículos de la BBC. "Health ministry", a
secas: 119. Para las cifras israelíes no existe la etiqueta.* — F05 via CfMM (the tag's
own count) + AlShebli et al. for the absence of "Knesset-run".
**Withheld:** what the numbers are; they are just numbers wearing or not wearing the tag.
**Why here:** `tag` establishes as a fresh figure (`metonimia-propietario`: the tag for
the doubt). From here the colour belongs to it.

### 5 — Pair 3. The guidance and the pages.
**On the frame:** two documents on one desk, same composition as frame 3: the BBC's own
guidance line ("terrorist" only with attribution) set in the house typeface; beside it, a
stack of pages with the word "terrorist" repeated in small type, 560 times implied by the
stack's height. A tiny `tag` in the corner.
**Caption:** *La guía de la BBC: "terrorist" solo con atribución. Sus páginas, oct–dic
2023: "terrorist" 560 veces, "militant" 488.* — F04, El Masry et al. 2025, BBC alone.
**Withheld:** any editor. The institution is its two texts.
**Why here:** the newsroom's own say/do; the `antifrasis` written by the subject.

### 6 — Inset. The count.
**On the frame:** a large grey newsroom wall; inset into it, small and sharp, the chart:
two bars, 16.1 and 0.36, the Gaza bar in the tag's colour. Nothing else in the frame moves.
**Caption:** *Primeros cien días de cada guerra. Menciones en antena por cada niño
muerto: Ucrania 16,1 · Gaza 0,36.* — F01.
**Withheld:** the raw numbers — still.
**Why here:** the H&C hinge, the table. `hierarchy: kernel`. The `inset` forces the stop.

### 7 — Braid-return. The rupture — the raw pair.
**On the frame:** the same chart, same site as frame 6 (Groensteen's iconic parallel),
and beside the two bars, small, two more: 4,223 and 3,632. They are almost the same
height. The tag's colour has moved to the small bars.
**Caption (the one overt voice):** *Las menciones brutas solo difieren un 33 %. El 45× sale
del denominador. Hay que decirlo antes de que lo diga otro: normalizar por muertos no es un
detalle técnico. Es la tesis.* — F01, the author against his own number.
**Withheld:** nothing. This is the frame that shows its work.
**Why here:** `mode` flips to surprise; the reader re-reads frame 6; the piece's honesty
is its authority. `carrier: mind` appears once.

### 8 — Beat. The form.
**On the frame:** a desk, seen from above (subject-to-subject from 7 if the desk is kept).
Eight sheets, fanned; on the top one a signature line and, in the tag's colour, a small
stamp. Beside them a lanyard. No hands. The most textured frame in the piece
(`textura-materica-impasta` on the paper) — the thumb's stop.
**Caption:** *Para entrar en Gaza, un periodista extranjero firma ocho páginas: entrada
solo escoltado, sin moverse solo, todo el material a la censura militar antes de usarlo.
CNN y NBC lo aceptaron y lo dijeron.* — F03, the IDF letter (8 pages), CNN/NBC caveats
verbatim; raw footage, not final reports.
**Withheld:** the correspondent, the escort, the crossing.
**Why here:** the mechanism, as an object. Pressure release before the landing.
`register`'s one poetic frame.

### 9 — Link. The rivals.
**On the frame:** three small cards in a row (`acumulacion-iconica`): a CNN logo-less
page, a calendar, two channel dials. Plain.
**Caption:** *Tres objeciones que caben: CNN usa la terminología al revés (1.242
"terrorist", 1.583 "militant"). Dos cadenas no son "la prensa". El 7 de octubre fue un
ataque filmado; Gaza, una campaña larga.* — El Masry et al.; the draft's §6.
**Withheld:** the rebuttal. The objections stand in the piece, as H&C stand theirs.
**Why here:** the rivals beat; the fairness the third circle needs to forward it.

### 10 — Terminal. The sentence.
**On the frame:** frame 1's two shelves, now seen as two lines of type — the composition
rhymes (Groensteen's terminal). The top line is a full sentence. The bottom line reads
*fueron matados ___* and the blank is the `hole` from frame 1. In the blank, faint, the
tag — or not: **decision for the brief** (see `04-brief.md`): the tag inside the hole
(the doubt fills the silence) or the tag absent (the hole finally unlabelled).
**Caption:** *¿Qué frase sin sujeto has leído hoy?* — the `discussion-hook`. Then, in the
first comment: the link to the annotated version on samuel-garijo.com.
**Withheld:** the answer.
**Why here:** Q&B's safe landing (settled) + Golden's open thread + Lukács's question
"posed as a question only". `hole` breaks (it becomes the reader's), `tag` breaks.

## The contagion link — where it goes, and its condition

Scope decision 4 allows *one* sourced mention of the Israeli rhetoric the Western
language echoes (F08). It belongs in frame 9's neighbourhood, as the mechanism's cause —
but the quotes are third-party and unverified. **Not in v1.** If verified (Herzog, 13 Oct
2023; the Law for Palestine database), it enters as a second caption line on frame 8 or as
an eleventh frame between 8 and 9. Until then the piece is B without C, which the draft
already judged sufficient.

## What the forge learned (for the `forge-story` skill)

- The H&C skeleton mapped cleanly onto Q&B's acts and Groensteen's roles; the only
  friction was the closure budget, which a pair-built genre exceeds by construction —
  calibrate per genre.
- The `late-fact` had to be *withheld across four frames* (1 → 7) while still being
  verifiable — the skill must track what has been shown and refuse a caption that leaks
  it early.
- Colour reserved for one symbol did more structural work than any figure: it turned the
  braid into something the reader can't miss. Worth a rule in `visual-rhetoric.md`.
