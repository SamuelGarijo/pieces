## Decisions taken (and why)

- **The piece is about B, framing — not A, attention.** The draft's own call (§3): "el
  texto es sobre B"; the Donbás hole is the hook, not the body. The summary widened into
  §3 (justification, Bandura, the "no innocents" quotes). Recorded as F08 with the scope
  question left to Samuel (see `02-strategy.md`).
- **Two US channels are not "Western media".** The draft's counter-argument #2. The
  summary generalizes; the piece won't. Every claim carries its sample.
- **No victim photographs.** The draft §8: illustrating a piece about the
  instrumentalization of victims' images with victims' images commits what it denounces.
  The illustration is the data, the grammar, the objects of the mechanism. This is a
  constraint on step 6, stated now.
- **Personal repo.** The piece is political and in Samuel's voice; the engine's ownership
  rule puts it here, not in the org repo.
