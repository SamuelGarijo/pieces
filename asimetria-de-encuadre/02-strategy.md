## Scope — decision 4, answered by the Perplexity conversation itself

"Alcance" meant *what the piece is about*: B (how coverage frames — the measurable
asymmetry) versus B+C (plus the justification rhetoric — "no hay inocentes", Bandura —
and the comparability/provocation debate). The full Perplexity conversation
(`00-sources/perplexity-conversation-2026-09.md`, 17 turns) answers it in Samuel's own
arc:

- It *started* as C — "¿se pueden comparar las provocaciones de Hamás con las de Ucrania?"
  — and spent ten turns on Donbás, Minsk, Mearsheimer, minorities, Euromaidán.
- Turn 12 is the turn: "empiezo a pensar que las comparaciones son bastante difíciles de
  mantener… el tema no es tanto quién tiene la razón, sino: los medios, cómo están
  justificando la no cobertura."
- Turn 13 asks the bridge question: does the Israeli "no innocents" rhetoric *transfer* to
  BBC/CNN — "¿se han contagiado?" — and gets the quantitative answer (selective doubt,
  terminology, mentions per death).
- The last turn fixes the scope: "no quiero meterme en temas históricos en esta
  publicación. Lo que sí me parece súper interesante es la duda selectiva de la BBC y la
  contaminación de la dialéctica israelí en los medios occidentales. Los datos
  cuantitativos… esa parte de hemeroteca, la más demostrable, me interesa mucho
  mantenerla."

**Scope, settled:** **B, plus the contagion link** — the piece shows the measurable
asymmetry (F01, F02, F04, F05, with F06 as hook and F03 as the structural cause) and
names, once and sourced, the Israeli rhetoric that the Western language echoes (F08's
quotes, third-party, verified before use). It does **not** litigate the provocation
debate, Mearsheimer, Euromaidán or the comparability of the two wars — those are the
conversation's scaffolding, not its result, and the draft's counter-argument #4 stands:
the object is newsrooms, not victims. The Donbás appears only as the hook ("el hueco").

Provenance note on the Perplexity file: it is the *lead list* the executive summary was
condensed from. Its citations look dense (footnoted URLs), but the verification pass
already showed the condensation compressed every figure it touched. Its links are useful
as a map to primaries; its numbers are not used without re-anchoring.

## What the strategy pass learned (for the `forge-story` skill)

  supplied. The skill must refuse to proceed to step 3 without a story sentence chosen.
- The grounding question needs the miner's `verified:` field or it can't be answered.
- Scope drift between two archive sources is a *strategy* question, not an extraction
  one — the miner flags it (F10), the strategy pass decides.
