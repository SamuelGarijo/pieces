## Scope — the fourth decision, from the archive's own disagreement

The draft (Aug 27) says the piece is about **B, framing**, with the Donbás hole as hook.
The summary (Sep 6) adds **C, justification** — the "no hay inocentes" quotes, Bandura,
Channel 14 — and the comparability debate. C is heavier, third-party-quoted, and turns
the piece from *how coverage works* into *why the violence is justified*. The draft's
counter-argument #4 ("esto relativiza el 7 de octubre — el objeto de estudio son las
redacciones, no las víctimas") is a warning against exactly that drift.

**Recommendation (mine):** B only for this piece. C is a second piece, with its own
verification burden and its own protagonist. **DECISION 4.**
